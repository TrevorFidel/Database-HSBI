# Course Reflections — Database Module

This section contains my personal reflections on the Database course sessions at Hochschule Bielefeld (HSBI), as part of the Mechatronics and Automation Engineering programme.

Each reflection records key learning points, practical experiences, challenges, exploration, and interactions during a class session. The aim is to document my learning progress, identify areas for improvement, and capture lessons that I can apply in future database projects.

## Reflection Guidelines

Each session reflection is based on the following questions:

1. **Take-away:** What are the most important points I learned? What do I want to remember, and what advice would I give based on the session?
2. **What was good?** Which activities, explanations, or exercises were particularly useful?
3. **What was difficult?** Where did I struggle, and what did I find challenging?
4. **Exploration and problem-solving:** What interesting experiments or discoveries came from struggling with a task? Were there any obstacles that required help from peers or instructors?
5. **Interaction with peers and instructors:** How did I collaborate, communicate, and exchange ideas during the session?
6. **Help and extra work:** Who helped me, and whose work did I support? Was feedback exchanged? Did I contribute through a presentation, implementation, pull request, or another activity that supported the class?
